# Trailhead

A simple job application tracker that runs in your browser. Log the jobs you apply to, keep track of follow-ups, store your resumes, and build cover letters from reusable templates.

No sign-up, no server, no cost. Everything is saved on your own device and never sent anywhere.

## What it does

- **Track applications** — company, role, status, date, salary, location, link, and notes, all in one table you can sort and search.
- **Quick paste** — paste a job posting and it tries to auto-fill the role, company, location, and salary for you.
- **Follow-up reminders** — anything you applied to and haven't heard back on gets flagged so you remember to check in.
- **Cover letters** — save your own cover letter as a template with `{{company}}`, `{{role}}`, and any other `{{blanks}}` you want. Pick a job, fill in the blanks, and you've got a letter ready to copy or download.
- **Documents** — keep your resumes and other files in one place.
- **Export** — download everything as an Excel spreadsheet anytime, or back it up as a file you can re-import later.

## How to use it

Open `index.html` in any browser. That's it — start adding jobs.

## Note

Your data is stored in your browser, so clearing your browser data will remove it. Use the Export buttons in Settings to keep a backup.
